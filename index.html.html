Official Pakistan textbooks & free PDFs

Open the official book portals below. Choose your class, subject and language to read or download available books.

📘PUNJAB · PRE-I–XII

Punjab Curriculum & Textbook Authority

Official Punjab e-books for primary, middle, secondary and higher-secondary classes. Browse books by class and subject.

📗SINDH · ECCE–XII

Sindh Textbook Board eBooks

Official free digital textbooks for all classes from Katchi to XII, in Sindhi, Urdu and English medium. The portal lists 194+ books.

Read / Browse ↗PDF Books ↗
📙KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA · KG–XII

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Textbook Board

Official KP Textbook Board information and textbook publication portal for school classes. Check the board site for current editions and availability.

Official Board ↗Books & Details ↗
📕BALOCHISTAN · GRADES 1–10

Balochistan Curriculum & Textbooks

Official School Education Department page for grade-wise curriculum and prescribed textbooks published with the Balochistan Textbook Board.

Read / Browse ↗Available e-Books ↗
📓FEDERAL BOARD · SSC / HSSC

Federal Board (FBISE) e-Library

FBISE's official e-library, syllabus and technical education book pages. Includes selected downloadable technical textbooks and study documents.

Open e-Library ↗PDF Technical Books ↗
🎓BS · UNIVERSITY LEVEL

OpenStax Free University Textbooks

Free, peer-reviewed, openly licensed university textbooks in mathematics, science, computer science, business, humanities and social sciences. Online reading and PDF downloads are available on book pages.

Read Online ↗Find Free PDFs ↗
🏛️PAKISTAN · RESEARCH

HEC National Digital Library

Scholarly journals, research databases and academic resources for eligible university and institutional users. Access depends on the institution's HEC subscription.

Read Access Details ↗Institution Portal ↗
📚FEDERAL · NBF

National Book Foundation

Government of Pakistan publisher with textbook chapters, selected online books and an official publication catalogue. Not every title is a free full PDF.

Read Online ↗Textbook Chapters ↗
About access: These buttons open the publishers' or education departments' own portals; they do not copy copyrighted books onto this website. On each portal, select the class and book, then use its own Read, View or Download option where provided. Some sources publish only selected titles or chapters.
